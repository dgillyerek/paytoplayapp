# Seraphine attacks, 2026-11-09

Two new clips on `SERAPHINE_rig`. The older throw and thrust actions were not used.

- `attack_crescent`: the spear opens out to her right, then cuts across the front to her left. The blade is in front of her at the middle of the clip.
- `attack_rise`: the spear drops to a low point in front of her right side, then rips upward through the space in front of her. The blade is high and in front at the middle of the clip.

Both start and end on the same guard so they can loop. 30 fps, 48 frames. Blender 5.2.

`attack_standing_melee` is Mixamo's Standing Melee Attack Horizontal, taken from the Lyra download and retargeted onto this rig. Shin and foot bones are `UpLeg.001` and `UpLeg.002`. 73 frames. Open `SERAPHINE_standing_melee.blend` and press Space.

Source: `SERAPHINE_attacks.blend`, built by `author_attacks.py`.
`attack_armada` is Mixamo's Armada, a capoeira spin kick. Downloaded without skin as FBX for Unity, 30 fps, keyframe reduction none. In Place was not in the download dialog. 50 frames. Shin and foot bones are `UpLeg.001` and `UpLeg.002`. Open `SERAPHINE_armada.blend` and press Space.

Unity preview: first attack is `SERAPHINE_attack_armada.fbx`, second is `SERAPHINE_attack_thrust_slash.fbx`. Standing melee and the rise clip stay on disk and are not in the preview list.

`attack_thrust_slash` is Mixamo's Thrust Slash. Downloaded without skin as FBX for Unity, 30 fps, keyframe reduction none. In Place was not in the download dialog. 91 frames. The spear stays on the right hand. Open `SERAPHINE_thrust_slash.blend` and press Space.
