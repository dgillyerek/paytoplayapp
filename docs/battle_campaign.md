# Battle campaign (generated chapters)

Derek 2026-11-02: "we need many more battle levels. it should progress with the rest of the game. stronger foes etc."

- 6 hand-made **Prologue** stages (unchanged), then **12 chapters x 10 stages = 120** generated stages (126 total).
- All numbers live in `campaign.json` -> `battle_campaign`; the stages are computed by `Runtime/Campaign/BattleCampaign.cs`.
- Stage *n* of 120: foe level = 8 + 42 x t^1.3 (t = 0 at stage 1, 1 at stage 120), so Lv 8 -> 50; stars 1 -> 5 in four even steps.
- Stages 1-4 have 2 foes, then always 3. Each chapter draws from a villain pool that starts with Nightfang, Ironhowl, Bonequill and adds one villain per chapter (Blightroot, Vespera, then Ashwyrm from chapter 4 on).
- Every 5th stage is a boss: the chapter's boss villain in the middle lane at +2 levels and +1 star, double gold, +60 gems, 4 shards of a buyable hero, 15 min of speed-ups. Stage 10 is the chapter Boss, stage 5 an Elite.
- First-clear rewards: gold 2,000 x 1.04^(n-1), food 40% of that, wood and stone 120% each, 20 gems. Replays pay 25% of the resources and no gems or shards.
- Chapter gates: every stage needs the one before it cleared, and the chapter's Castle level: 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,14,16,18.
- Clearing a whole chapter unlocks a FREE chapter chest (gold, gems, speed-ups), claimed once.

## Pull on the runner pace

The campaign is **off** in the locked pace model (`BattleCampaign = Off`), so runner days are byte-identical to main.
With campaign first-clear rewards paid into the sim (`Grant` mode): free L31 day 220.33 -> 215.33 (-2.3%), L50 never -> never (same as main). Gold/food was the only part that moved it, which is why stage gold is kept small and most of the payout is wood/stone (buildings) and gems/speed-ups.
